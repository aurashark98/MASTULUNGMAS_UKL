Create a complete premium responsive website design from scratch for an Indonesian digital platform called "Mas Tulung Mas (MTM)".

PROJECT BACKGROUND

Mas Tulung Mas is a modern digital gotong royong platform that connects people who need help with trusted local helpers called "Mitra Tulung".

The platform enables users to request assistance for everyday tasks such as:

* Delivery and courier services
* Personal assistance
* Queueing services
* Shopping assistance
* Home repairs
* Technical support
* Elderly assistance
* Custom tasks

The platform represents the modernization of Indonesian gotong royong culture through technology.

The design should feel like the future of community assistance in Indonesia.
